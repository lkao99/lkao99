### Hi, I'm Lawrence 👋

I'm building **40 apps in 40 days**, live on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/40for40) from London with [Samira Sohail](https://github.com/samirasohail) and [Ben Bourdin](https://github.com/ben-bourdin451). That's 40 hackathons back to back. Every weekday at 10:00 a problem from the community gets revealed, and by the end of the day it's a real app with a link you can open. Or it sinks, and we mark it shipwrecked. Both happen.

Everything lives at **[build40for40.com](https://build40for40.com)**, and the code is in the [build40for40](https://github.com/build40for40) org.

### What I do on the team

I make the apps look and feel like something you'd want to use: brand, landing pages, design and front end. Most days that means:

- **Brand and identity** for each app, from the name and logo to the colours and type
- **Landing pages** that explain the app to someone who's never heard of it
- **Front end and UI polish**, shipped as lots of small pull requests so the team can build on them the same day
- **The 40 for 40 site itself**, from the day board to the font
